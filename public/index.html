Print & Book Assembly Instructions

Follow these sequential instructions precisely to bind your individual booklets into a single, cohesive book:

1
Double-Sided Printing
Download each booklet PDF. Print each file independently. In your printer dialog, select Double-Sided Printing (Duplex) and choose "Flip on Short Edge" (Landscape flip).
2
Fold and Staple
Keep sheets organized by booklet. Fold each stack in half at the center. Use a booklet stapler (saddle-stitcher) to put 2 staples down the center fold lines of each booklet independently.
3
Stack and Bind
Stack the folded booklets together in chronological order: Booklet #1 on top, followed by Booklet #2, #3, etc. Stitch their spines together or glue them to form your final book spine.
